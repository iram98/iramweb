  • Ask the GatorAI agent ↗

    A research agent that tells you the evolutionary story behind any trait.

    read more

    How it works:

    1. Claude picks 1–3 example species
    2. Paperclip finds 20 papers for each
    3. Claude scores them on my criteria and keeps the best 8
    4. Claude reads them and pulls out cited evidence
    5. you get a TLDR and a scientific version of the story

    Gator takes away the initial friction of finding sources. I've worked in labs for the last 5 years, and I know how much time and patience science takes. AI makes information easier to reach, which helps with some parts of that process.
