  • Ask the GatorAI agent ↗

    An evolutionary research assistant. Ask about any trait, like why humans get goosebumps, and it tells you that trait's evolutionary story.

    read more

    I'm still wondering which parts of science could be automated.

    • is it the questions?
    • is it finding relevant sources?
    • is it putting the data together?

    When you ask Gator a question:

    1. Claude picks 1–3 example species
    2. Paperclip finds 20 papers for each
    3. Claude scores them on my criteria and keeps the best 8
    4. Claude reads them and pulls out cited evidence
    5. you get a TLDR and a scientific version of the story

    The initial friction of finding sources is lowered.

    I've worked in multiple labs over the last 5 years, and I know the scientific process takes a lot of time and patience. AI helps with access to information, which is helpful for some parts of the process.
